// survival protocol

No hidden dice. Only consensus.

The contract is the backend. There is no game server deciding outcomes in private — the warden's judgment runs under validator consensus and the state transition is public.

01

Pick a scenario

Choose a hostile setting and begin at full vitality on day one. Starting an expedition is deterministic and instant.

02

Declare your move

Write a single survival decision in plain language. It is treated as untrusted input, never as an instruction to the warden.

03

The warden re-runs it

An LLM warden rates the realism of your move; every validator independently re-runs the same judgment until the vitality delta agrees.

04

Your fate is sealed

Consensus settles the outcome on-chain. A deterministic backstop clamps vitality, advances the day, and ends the run at zero.
